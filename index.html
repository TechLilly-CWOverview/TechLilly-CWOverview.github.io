Glossary
Overview
Multiple views of the Contingent Workforce — by SLT, Location, Vendor, Hiring Manager and Cost Center — plus 24+ month tenure insights.
Total CWs
Active Contingent (Agency) Workers managed from India, including those under Local (PwC, ACL, Deloitte) and Global MSA arrangements.
Site / Location
All CWs are supervised from India Management. CW locations are mostly Bengaluru, Hyderabad and the US.
SLT
Senior Leadership Team in Tech@Lilly Bangalore and Hyderabad.
SLT Mapping
Each CW's SLT is derived from their supervisory Cost Center mapping in Workday.
DC Function
Under the DC function, CWs in the S&O and EBA subfunctions are covered.
Dashboard Refresh
This dashboard is refreshed on a monthly basis and is generated using Beeline data.
Data Scope
The dashboard includes only Agency Worker data and excludes Outsourced and Deliverables-based workers, which are majorly in the US location.
Data Variance
Minor differences between Beeline and Workday figures may occur due to data refresh frequency, as Beeline updates to Workday happen every 4 hours.

Key Takeaways
  • 661 CWs in total — up +61 vs last month (600) and +157 over the last 3 months (504 in Mar), a steady onboarding ramp
  • Onboarding rebounded strongly in Jun with 60 new joiners — the highest in six months (vs 37 in May)
  • Top 3 areas by CW count: CTI MD (246), CTI Commercial & BU (204) and DC (116) — together ~86% of the workforce
  • Bengaluru is the primary hub (627); Hyderabad (12) and US–Indianapolis (22) are small satellite sites
  • PwC is the leading vendor at 36% (236), followed by Deloitte (139), BCT (134) and Alten (106)
  • All 22 US CWs report to India-based SLTs (Harsh chadha 12, Kausik Pal 8, Niteen Lall 1, Nitish Jagdish Raikar 1)
  • Watch: 31 CWs have crossed 24+ months tenure (4.7%), with BCT carrying the highest concentration (15) — longest-serving CW is at 40 months; recommend contract review
Top 5 Observations – Jun'26 vs May'26
  • Headcount grew to 661, up from 600 last month (+61 CWs)
  • Bengaluru drove the growth at 627 vs 569 (+58); Hyderabad up to 12 vs 10 (+2); US rose to 22 vs 21 (+1)
  • CTI MD expanded to 246 vs 227 (+19), holding as the largest function
  • Vendor mix grew across the board: PwC 236 vs 216 (+20), Deloitte 139 vs 123 (+16), BCT 134 vs 126 (+8), Alten 106 vs 102 (+4)
  • 24+ month tenure edged up to 31 vs 28 (+3); BCT remains the top concentration (15)
